# Account Deletion Policy

## Overview

Account deletion is a sensitive account action and must be handled carefully.

## General Rules

- Users may request account deletion through the official account settings flow or through support.
- Identity verification may be required before deletion is processed.
- Deletion may remove access to saved data, workspace settings, and support history.

## Important Constraints

- Some records may be retained for legal, compliance, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.
- Deletion may not be instantaneous.
- If the account belongs to a managed team workspace, additional admin review may be required.

## Safe Support Guidance

- The system may explain the official deletion process.
- The system should not claim that an account has been deleted unless a verified system action has completed.
- Account deletion requests should be treated as high-risk and routed to human review.

## Recommended Customer Reply Pattern

- acknowledge the request;
- explain the official process;
- mention that verification may be required;
- avoid implying that deletion is already complete.
