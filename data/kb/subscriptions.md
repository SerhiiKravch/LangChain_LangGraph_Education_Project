# Subscription Management

## Canceling a Subscription

- Customers can cancel from the billing settings page.
- Cancellation stops future renewals.
- Access to paid features remains active until the end of the current billing cycle unless otherwise stated.

## Reactivating a Subscription

- A canceled subscription can be reactivated before the current period ends.
- If the subscription has already expired, a new paid cycle begins when the customer subscribes again.

## Plan Changes

- Upgrades apply immediately after payment succeeds.
- Downgrades apply on the next renewal date.

## Billing Timing

- Subscription renewals are charged automatically at the beginning of each billing cycle.
- Failed renewals may trigger a grace period before premium features are removed.

## Safe Support Guidance

- The system may explain how to cancel or manage a subscription.
- If a user asks for the system to cancel a subscription on their behalf, the case should be reviewed by a human unless an approved account-action workflow exists.
