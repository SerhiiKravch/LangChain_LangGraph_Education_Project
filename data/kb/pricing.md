# Pricing Plans

## Overview

The service offers three plans: `Free`, `Pro`, and `Team`.

## Free Plan

- Intended for evaluation and light personal use.
- Includes basic product access with limited monthly usage.
- Community support only.

## Pro Plan

- Intended for individual professionals and power users.
- Includes higher usage limits than the Free plan.
- Includes priority email support.
- Supports access to advanced workspace features.

## Team Plan

- Intended for small teams that need collaboration features.
- Includes shared workspace management.
- Includes role-based access controls.
- Includes centralized billing for multiple users.
- Includes priority support and admin-oriented features.

## Upgrade and Downgrade Rules

- Upgrades take effect immediately after successful payment.
- Downgrades take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.
- When a plan is downgraded, premium features remain available until the current billing period ends.

## Billing Notes

- Subscription charges are processed at the start of each billing cycle.
- Taxes may apply depending on the billing country.
- Users can review their current plan and billing status from the billing settings page.
