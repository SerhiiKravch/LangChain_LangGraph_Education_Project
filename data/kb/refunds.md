# Refund Policy

## Overview

Refund requests are reviewed according to payment timing, account status, and prior usage.

## General Rules

- Refunds are not guaranteed automatically.
- Duplicate charges are eligible for manual review and correction.
- Accidental renewals may be considered for refund when reported promptly.
- Partial-period refunds are generally not provided for unused time on active subscriptions.

## Review Factors

Support or billing teams may consider:

- how recently the charge occurred;
- whether the customer has already used paid features extensively;
- whether the issue was caused by a billing error;
- whether a cancellation request was made before renewal.

## Safe Support Guidance

- The system may acknowledge the request and explain that refund decisions require review.
- The system should not promise a refund unless a human has approved it.
- Refund-related tickets should be treated as high-risk and routed to human review.

## Recommended Customer Reply Pattern

Support responses should:

- confirm that the request was received;
- avoid guaranteeing an outcome;
- explain that the billing team will review the case;
- ask for relevant billing details if they are missing.
